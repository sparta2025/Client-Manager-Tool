---
name: CRM team access
description: User-selected roles and case visibility for the lawyer CRM.
---

Use three roles: administrator, lawyer, and assistant. Administrators can see all cases; lawyers and assistants can see only cases assigned to them.

**Why:** the user chose role-based access and assigned-case-only visibility for the team.

**How to apply:** keep case access scoped on the server, including detail, journal, AI, and reminder endpoints, and preserve the same visibility rule in the UI.
